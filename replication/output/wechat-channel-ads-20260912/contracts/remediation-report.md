## 1. What was wrong (WHY)

The historical keyframe prompt JSONs for products 01/02/04/05/06 each carried two review
blocks — `h3_prompt_review` (`skill:"h3-prompt-writing"`) and `seedance_prompt_review`
(`skill:"seedance-prompt-zh"`) — both stamped:

- `authorship: "generated_by_skill"`
- `result: "pass"`

These stamps were **hand-filled by the orchestrator**, not produced by any skill's
generate-before-review. They were emitted as hard-coded constants by the self-stamping
generator scripts in `tmp/` (see §4). A stamp that is authored by hand and then labeled
`generated_by_skill` / `pass` is forged provenance: it makes an ungenerated, unverified
prompt masquerade as "generated by a skill and passed review before generation." This
violates 三-skill-SOP §112.

Per the honesty-correction mandate, the evidence is **not deleted** — the review blocks are
kept in place and truthfully **downgraded** so they can no longer masquerade as gate-passed.
