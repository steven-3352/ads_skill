## Creative premise

A clean, slightly roguish man prepares to leave in the morning. Five real-looking animals reveal engineered internal structures and reconfigure into fashion accessories.

Core transformation rule:

```text
photoreal animal -> recognizable mechanical animal -> finished wearable object
```

The transformation is never organic melting or body compression. Fur, feathers and scales separate as clean articulated shells; internal rails, hinges and clasps rotate and lock into place.

## Locked visual system

- Format: vertical 9:16 fashion TVC.
- Setting: bright contemporary dressing room at dawn; warm white plaster, pale limestone, clear glass and controlled cobalt accents.
- Base outfit: cobalt-blue cropped technical jacket, ivory ribbed knit, charcoal high-waisted straight trousers.
- Product family: graphite black, champagne silver and cobalt anodized-metal accents.
- Lighting: realistic high-key morning sunlight, clean highlights, rich but believable color.
- Human strategy: body crops during transformations; complete man appears only in the final hero frame.
- No brands, logos, typography or recognizable franchise design language.
